Thirty levels and four new mechanics built on top of HeavyLight, finished in less time than the original took. That was the whole point of building it.

Playable here. Also on itch.io.

Why it exists

I wrote HeavyLight as a base rather than a one-off, which is easy to claim and hard to prove. Conclusus was the test: take the same foundation, build something bigger on it, and see whether the second project is cheaper than the first or whether the "reusable" code quietly costs more than starting over.

The result

Twenty levels became thirty. Four mechanics that didn't exist in the original went in on top of the light-weight rule without rewriting it. And it shipped faster, which is the number that actually settles the question.

What I'd change

Reuse worked at the systems level and not at the content level — every level was still hand-placed from nothing. The next version of this argument would put the same effort into authoring tools that went into the mechanics.
