# Research 01: inside the womb (fixthemothering)

Digest for the new brief (2026-09-26): the camera goes inside the flesh
ball and tells the story from the soup to the calm floating womb. The
takeaways are in our own words, and no text or art is copied. Every
direction must still pass `.claude/rules/art-style.md`:
- flat fills with a left-lit `litShade` split
- held steps and snaps, never morphs
- tier 3 never whole
- a dressed body is a new asset
- the rAF loop is the cost

## 1. Inside palette, primordial soup (red eats blue)

Sources:
- Lennart Nilsson, *A Child Is Born* (1965).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Child_Is_Born_(book) and
  https://embryo.asu.edu/pages/child-born-1965-lennart-nilsson
  - Inside the body reads warm and translucent: amber, rose and dark maroon, with the subject lit against the dark.
- Spore, Cell Stage. https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage
  - A top-down 2D ooze where eating is growing; debris sits in parallax layers; the predator swells visibly as it feeds.
- Scavengers Reign art direction, after Moebius.
  https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/scavengers-reign-animated-series-influences-art-1235623762/
  and https://www.charleshuettner.com/work/scavengers-reign
  - Alien biology in flat colour blocks with no gradients, the closest match to our house style.
- Annihilation, the Shimmer VFX.
  https://vfxblog.com/2018/03/12/mandelbulbs-mutations-and-motion-capture-the-visual-effects-of-annihilation/
  - Growth patterns come from cell division; the mutation shows as a pattern that spreads over surfaces.
- Hilma af Klint, *Primordial Chaos* (1906-07).
  https://artblart.com/tag/hilma-af-klint-primordial-chaos/ and
  https://www.modernamuseet.se/en/malmo/exhibitions/hilma-af-klint/in-depth/
  - Creation painted as two opposed colours with spirals and seed forms; a flat, diagrammatic way to show a cosmic process.
- Evangelion, LCL. https://wiki.evageeks.org/LCL
  - A single warm primordial fluid that bodies dissolve back into.

Directions:
- Scavengers Reign: the soup is 3 flat value layers of maroon to rose blobs, lit from the left. No gradient, even inside.
- Spore cell stage: the red blobs grow a held step each time they swallow a blue one, and the blue count visibly drops.
- Af Klint: blue is only a few cool accents (5-10% of the area), so each loss reads.
- Nilsson: the backdrop turns from #0d1114 to a deep oxblood near-black, the one warm "inside" field of the cutscene. The caption still sits on it at full contrast.
- Annihilation: the red spreads over a blue blob as a stepped ring of cells, not a fade.

## 2. The eldritch eye: dust, colour separation, dead dots, colour point

Sources:
- Avengers: Infinity War, the "blip" disintegration (Weta).
  https://gizmodo.com/how-wetas-vfx-team-brought-the-most-epic-moments-of-ave-1828264355
  and https://www.awn.com/vfxworld/weta-and-thanos-come-full-circle-avengers-endgame
  - A wave front runs across the body and flakes peel off and drift; a cheaper "lite" version is used for background figures.
- Paper chromatography. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_chromatography
  - One mix splits into its pigments, which travel at different rates. The name means "colour writing".
- Rothko's colour fields.
  https://www.artsy.net/article/artsy-editorial-mark-rothko-unlocked-emotional-power-color
  - Colour as a free-floating mass with no form holding it.
- Bloodborne, the Moon Presence and Great Ones' insight.
  https://bloodborne.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Presence
  - A vast watcher that is felt more than seen; seeing it changes the viewer.

Directions:
- Bloodborne, obeying tier 3 (D8): the eye is only a lidless arc cut by the frame edge, 1-2 steps off the backdrop. It opens in 3 held steps, and nothing beams from it.
- Weta's blip: a stepped wave front crosses the soup left to right. Behind it every blob becomes grey dots, done in held rows, not a per-particle fade.
- Chromatography: each dot's colour lifts off as a small flat chip that rises. The grey dot stays behind, so there are two arrays from one.
- The dead dots fall in held steps to a floor line and pile up. That pile is the dust in area 4.
- Rothko: the rising chips snap together, a few per step, into one flat point. It stays the brightest thing in frame, as the mark rule asks.

## 3. Role bodies and clothes (enforcers, watchers, mothers, messengers)

Sources:
- Termite castes. https://www.britannica.com/animal/termite/Natural-history
  and https://genent.cals.ncsu.edu/bug-bytes/social-insects/termites/
  - One genome makes different bodies by task; a worker moults through a pre-soldier stage into a soldier with a huge head; workers are eyeless and pale; the queen is swollen.
- Royal jelly and bee castes. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_jelly
  - Diet alone decides queen or worker; the transformation is a new body, not a costume.
- Bloodborne, the Healing Church sets (Choir, Executioners, Black Church).
  https://bloodborne.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Church_Attire
  - One institution with ranks told apart by silhouette: the Choir's blindfold hoods, the Executioners' tall capotain hats, the Black Church's long capes.
- Moirai iconography. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fates
  - Three roles read by one prop each: spindle, rod, shears.
- Edward Gorey. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Gorey
  - Fur coats and long scarves on figures as dark, flat shapes; winter clothes read as mass, which matches our rule.

Directions:
- Termite pre-soldier: every old one snaps through one held intermediate body, then into its role body. Two snaps, never a morph, one phase per group (D9).
- Termite soldier: the enforcers get an oversized head or jaw mass plus a garment trait pushed past sense (a brim wider than the body, a huge cuirass-coat).
- Choir blindfolds: the watchers are the few with rare unique eyes, or eye holes cut through a hood. Tall, thin and still.
- Swollen queen: the mothers are low and wide, with bustles, capes and a ring cradle (shoulders built to hold rings). Scalloped fur mass.
- Moirai: the messengers carry the strand props (spindle, reel, trailing thread). Long shoes, lean bodies, scarves streaming. They run the strands in area 4.
- One caption names all four (D6). The groups stand apart by silhouette alone, per the "outline first" rule.

## 4. Building the circle: dust pile, swirling strands, the colour thread

Sources:
- Tyler Hobbs, "Flow Fields". https://www.tylerxhobbs.com/words/flow-fields
  - A grid of angles steers many curves; its ways to make them look good: avoid collisions, vary thickness, stop lines early.
- Accretion disks. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accretion_disk
  - Matter loses momentum and spirals inward, faster near the centre.
- Van Gogh, *The Starry Night*. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Starry_Night
  - Swirls built from many short parallel strokes.
- Moirai spinning (Clotho). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clotho
  - A single thread drawn out of a mass: the spun life.
- Chiharu Shiota's thread installations. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiharu_Shiota
  - Thousands of taut threads converge on one object; one red thread as fate.
- Attack on Titan, the Paths. https://attackontitan.fandom.com/wiki/Path
  - Countless thin paths converge into one luminous trunk; a sand-coloured ground where bodies are moulded from dust.

Directions:
- Attack on Titan Paths: the grey dust pile is swept toward the circle's rim in held steps, like a dune creeping over a floor.
- Accretion disk: 40-80 thin strands spiral inward and speed up near the rim.
  - Draw them as one path per bundle of 8-10 polylines, each strand a precomputed curve; only the drawn length grows each step.
- Hobbs: bake the strand curves once from a small angle grid at setup. Never trace them per frame.
- Clotho and Shiota: a single thread from the colour point to the circle, drawn out by a messenger. It is the one saturated line, and every other strand stays grey.
- Starry Night: the swirl reads as short, stepped, parallel strokes round the circle, never a glow. When it closes, the circle snaps to a flat disc with a litShade split: the first universe, dull and quiet.

## 5. Chains of rings, and scale up to billions

Sources:
- Eames, *Powers of Ten* (1977).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powers_of_Ten_(film_series)
  - Scale by steady zoom, one power of ten per interval; a frame square marks the last view.
- Indra's net. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra%27s_net
  - An infinite net with a jewel at every knot, each reflecting all the others. Many-as-one.
- Mail armour. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_(armour)
  - Each ring linked through four others. Countless small rings read as one fabric.
- Galaxy filaments. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_filament
  - At the largest scale the universe is a web of threads and knots.
- Dark Souls opening (linking the fire).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Souls_(video_game)
  - A cosmic cycle told in a few slow held shots.

Directions:
- Mail: each birth is a ring snapping out through the last one, interlinked, not touching. The first chain has 6-8 readable rings.
- Powers of Ten: a stepped zoom-out of 3-4 held jumps, each shrinking the view about 10x. The last jump's frame is one ring in the new view.
- Galaxy filaments: at the far scale the rings become a pre-rendered offscreen texture of chains, drawn once and stamped. It reads as threads with knots: billions implied, never drawn.
- Indra's net: magic-born rings carry one bright chip at a knot. Most stay dull, so the few "with magic" count.
- Dark Souls: many initial chains fan from the first universe like a root, held still between snaps.

## 6. Corruption: rings go red, are cut down, returned; the red goes up

Sources:
- Efferocytosis, the clearing of dead cells.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/emm201752
  - Dying cells signal to be found and eaten; the eater surrounds, sinks and digests them, and the contents are recycled.
- Cells at Work!. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cells_at_Work!
  - Cleanup as a job done by uniformed workers: roles as costumes in a body.
- Moirai, Atropos. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fates
  - Cutting the thread is its own role and its own tool.
- Kintsugi, as the opposite. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kintsugi
  - Repair makes the break visible and golden. We invert it: the break is removed.

Directions:
- A corrupted ring goes red in held steps: a stepped red arc spreads round it, reusing the look of the area-1 soup.
- Atropos: an enforcer's shears or blade cut its links. The ring drops out of the chain in one snap.
- Efferocytosis: mothers fold round the cut ring. It shrinks in steps back to grey dust, and the dust walks back to the pile from area 4 (the base process).
- The leftover red will not leave the dust, so it rises in held steps to a red layer against the womb roof. It thickens per ring: a count you can read.
- Anti-kintsugi: where a ring was cut, the chain keeps a clean gap, with no mark.

## 11. Cheap 2D canvas for many particles and strands

Sources:
- MDN, Optimizing canvas.
  https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Canvas_API/Tutorial/Optimizing_canvas
  - Pre-render repeated shapes offscreen; use integer coordinates; batch state changes and skip shadowBlur.
- Tyler Hobbs, "Flow Fields" (area 4).
  - Curves come from a precomputed grid, so they can be traced once and reused.
- Oskar Fischinger and Norman McLaren. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oskar_Fischinger
  and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_animation
  - Abstract motion from few elements with strict timing: rhythm sells the count.

Directions:
- Particle pools: one `Float32Array` per field (x, y, colour index) at a fixed size of about 800-1500 for the soup and dust. No objects per particle.
- Colour buckets: sort by colour index. Set `fillStyle` once per bucket, then do one `fillRect` per dot at integer pixels. No arcs for dots under 3 px.
- Held steps: update positions only on step ticks (6-12 per second), not per frame. That is cheaper and on-style, per the "stillness" rule.
- Strands: one `beginPath` and one `stroke` per bundle, with precomputed polylines. Growth is an index into the point list.
- Stamps: pre-render the rings and the far chain web to offscreen canvases once, then `drawImage` many times. The billions shot is one texture.
- Stop the soup and dust arrays when their beat ends, and skip everything off screen. The loop stays the bill (memory: bridge cost is the loop).
