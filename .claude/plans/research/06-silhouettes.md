# Phase 6 · Silhouettes: proportion pass

## Research digest (sources, and what we take)

- **Dishonored's people** (Game Informer 2011 "The Look and Feel of
  Dishonored", 2016 Mitton interview; go2games "Art of Dishonored: The
  Characters"): roles are read from pushed body parts (guards with big
  hands and small skulls), worked out with anatomy and silhouette
  specialists. We take: push the one part that says what the being is,
  shrink what does not matter.
- **Silhouette / shape-language guides** (80 Level "Shape Language and
  Readability", Rocketbrush, Lunar Academy, Adventure Gamers "Design in
  Silhouette", a sprite-readability lab on itch.io): fill the figure
  black and squint; broad shapes first, detail only where it helps the
  outline; negative space (gaps between legs, holes) defines form; test
  in grey, at distance and in motion. We take: the flat-fill sheet as the
  test, and gaps (under the strider, between the comb's teeth, inside the
  knot and chime) as part of the silhouette.
- **Edward Gorey**: spindly, calm, elongated figures; long against
  round. **Burton**: stretch the lanky further, contrast a big part on a
  thin one. **Mignola**: blocky, few shapes, maximum contrast, minimal
  rendering. We take: size contrast inside one body (one big bulb, one
  tall shard, one lead speck) and tall-thin versus low-wide pairs.

## What changed (Phase 6)

Pairs of opposites: the strider (tallest legs, body lifted) against the
crawler (flattened, legs splayed); the tower (thinner, sharper) against
the slab (wider, shorter, taller spikes for the gibus); the mound lower
and wider. Size contrast inside a body: bundle lobes 0.07–0.25, the
bloom's front bulb 1.6×, the prism's one tall shard, the swarmling's lead
speck 3×. Longer reach: needle, winged, floater tendrils, comb teeth,
veil drape, roller spikes, blinker top point, slider breath and mass
lobes.

Eyes kept on six, each its own: the eye (its pupil, bigger), the mound
(the big disc), the tower (a vertical slit), the strider (a horizontal
slit), the bundle (a pale ring), the husk (a small dot). Dropped on the
crawler, floater, slab, bloom, comb, chime, prism, swarmling, and the
mass's two dark holes. The colossus is untouched (Phase 8 replaces it).

Obrokxus's brothers (`makeOne`, depths) are born of the womb and out of
scope: `makeOne` marks them `o.bro` and the `pu(o, old, now)` helper in
`genesis-oldones.js` keeps their old proportions and eyes.
