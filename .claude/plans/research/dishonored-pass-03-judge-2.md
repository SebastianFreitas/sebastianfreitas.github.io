## bridge-void-unclaimed (desktop 1440x900)

The modes toggle (top-left) is a small black torn smudge with the icon: fine but tiny. The "HOLD ANYWHERE..." notice is on a black torn strip: yes.

## bridge-phone-landscape

- 2: **no**. The notice's headline, its biggest line, "The bridge needs the long side of your screen.", is a web sans, not the pause menu's cream serif. Colour is cream and there is no red, but the title does not read as the refs'.

Statement 3: not judged at this phase.

## Verdict: FAIL

Reasons, most important first:
1. Instrument tiles still read as web cards: a crisp grey 1 px outline around each box, and the stroke behind them (where it shows at all) is too dark and too faint to beat the outline.
2. Genesis progress track reads as a dashed web line, and the whole control row (track, pause, skip) is too small to read as painted pieces.
3. The portrait rotate notice's headline is in a plain web sans on the painted plate, so the plate's main title does not read as the pause menu's serif.
