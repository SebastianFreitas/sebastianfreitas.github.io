## Verdict: FAIL

## Top reasons (most important first)
1. Dirt and marks (S3). The wall is a flat, clean warm taupe everywhere, with no big strokes, scratches or vignette. The only marks are the section-title ink clusters: rounded cartoon puffs plus many tiny dots, i.e. fine noise rather than a few big rough marks. Gap: section wall/wash plus the ink cluster beside each section title (Work, Experience) and the one in the footer.
2. Type ladder misses (S2). Item headings in the Experience rows are bold sans. The footer's name and links are underlined sans web links. Neither reads as the cream serif of the menu. Gap: experience row role/title line; footer name, email and link row.
3. Section seam and cut-looking ends (S1). A hard, straight, full-width tone break between the Experience and footer sections turns the wash into web bands. Every slab is a flat fill whose ends are a regular repeated sawtooth, with no scrape lines between rows, so they read as cut paper rather than dragged paint. Gap: the boundary between the Experience section and the footer; the torn-end shape and fill of the work, experience and footer slabs.
