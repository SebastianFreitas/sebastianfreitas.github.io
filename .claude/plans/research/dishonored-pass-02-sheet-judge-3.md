- S2: yes.
- S3: **no.** The ink is a domed dripping-cloud stamp with a straight wedge tail, which also breaks D43's "no straight lines". The wash patches are round airbrush spotlights, not brushed paint.
- One-line fix: break the ink cluster into an irregular spray of mixed-size blots and flecks, with no domed top, no parallel drips and no wedge tail. Make the wash patches streaky, brush-shaped smears instead of round glows.

## Verdict: FAIL

Top reasons:
1. **Ink shape:** every cluster reads as a black dripping tree or mushroom cloud with a straight tail, not as splatter. The ref's ink is a spray of uneven dots.
2. **Wash shape:** the lighter patches are perfect soft circles (spotlights), not the refs' streaky painted wash.
3. **Repetition:** column C stamps the identical ink shape twice. The long edges of every plate are machine-clean, so only the ends look torn.

Passing cells: tears B only. Wash C passes statement 1 but fails 3. No ink cell passes.
