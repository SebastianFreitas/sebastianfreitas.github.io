# C25 build: the span line at the top

Owner on C24: "no should be closer to the original, a line that represents the full length of the map, with lines for places of interest we haven't seen yet".

Built by tmp/mk25.py: copies scripts/opt-c24b.js to opt-c25.js, swaps only the C24B top block (chevron, ring, name, pip row, count) for the span line, registers c25 in scripts/page.html. Render: `py -3 scripts/render.py c24b c25`, check: `py -3 tmp/chk25.py`.

- Data: as C23/C24 (the old centre track read off the base shot): span 408 km (CAM.min..CAM.max), 17 beacons, 10 read, 7 unread, you at 228 km. Nearest unread = 9 km west.
- Line: y 66.5, x 200..1800 (1600 px, ~3.9 px/km), wings' thin gold (GOLD, STRUCT .42), tiny end ticks at both ends of the span.
- Unseen beacons: gold ticks 7 px tall rising from the line (6 of them). Seen beacons: faint gold dots (r 1.2, a .3) on the line.
- Next one: the nearest unseen tick is cream, 10 px tall, with glow; "9 km" (size 10 cream) under the line, just left of it.
- You: small cream caret under the line pointing up at your spot, "you" (mic grey) beside it.
- No arrows, compass, rings or boxes. Top paint bbox (bare): x 200..1801, y 55..82.
- Diff vs C24B outside the top strip (y 48..180): 0 px, bare and full. Log centre and C22 wings unchanged.
- Note: the sheet's option tag ("C25 · span line ...") sits top-left over the line's west end in the full shot, as the C24 tags did; it is not part of the HUD.

Outputs: out/c25-full.png, dbg/c25-top.png (180..1820 x 44..96), dbg/c25-top-near.png (990..1190, 4x).
