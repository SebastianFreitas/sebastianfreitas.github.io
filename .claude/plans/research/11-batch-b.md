# Phase 11 research: wardrobe batch B (east, small + light)
Rows 10-17 of `05-wardrobe.md`. D28: each Dressed is a new body from scratch, never
`<kind>Bare` plus cloth. One flat fill per garment, one tell at 40-80 px. All misfit.

**Swarmling: gamp.** Bare: 12 orbiting specks, lead 3x. Gamp (Dickens 1844): bulgy,
loosely tied cotton umbrella; tell = baggy dome of 8 sagging scallops, ferrule on top.
Misfit: open, 3x the swarm, against a cold that does not fall. New body: lead speck
fuses 5 specks into a 0.9 hf mast; the other 11 hang still in a bell under it, one slot
snap per beat. Rule: canopy 3x bell width, 0.35x tall, dips 0.06x; navy; one panel 1 px low.

**Prism: veiled hat, one plume.** Bare: 4 shards fanned from a base point, tallest 1.25
hf. 1880s small tipped hat, net veil, one upright ostrich plume; tell = plume > hat.
Misfit: veil falls over the eye at the base, not a face. New body: shards fuse into one
upright obelisk, two stubs splay as a foot, one eye-facet low. Rule: hat 0.25x obelisk
at the tip; plume 2x hat, plum, flat curl; mourning veil to the base, 5-7 holes in a grid.

**Bloom: poke bonnet.** Bare: a low 1.2 hf lobed slug, 5 pulsing bulbs on top. Poke
(1800-50): flat crown, deep brim hiding the face; tell = the tunnel. Misfit: tied on one
bulb, four still pulse bare. New body: front bulb rises on a 0.6 hf stalk, a swan neck;
slug shortens to 0.7 hf; four bulbs shrink to a row on the back. Rule: brim 3x bulb,
1.5x bulb forward, camel, inside one tone dark; two oxblood tails 2x stalk, 1 frame late.

**Veil: Inverness cape.** Bare: a floating wavy translucent band 1.8 hf wide, 3 fringe
strands. Inverness (1880s): sleeveless, arm holes under a shoulder cape; tell = stepped
double hem. Misfit: heavy cape slipping off the lightest body. New body: the band hangs
vertical from a shoulder line like a sheet on a peg, 0.9 x 0.6 hf, 0.75 alpha. Rule: cape
opaque tweed, tiers 0.4x and 0.7x body, lower 2x wide; tipped 12 deg, right 0.15 hf low.

**Blinker: bowler.** Bare: a kite diamond 0.64 hf, a pale blink bar. Bowler (Lock & Co,
1849): hard low dome, narrow curled brim; tell = dome plus rolled brim. Misfit: 2x too
wide, drops to mid-body, lands a beat late per hop. New body: diamond stretches to 1.0 hf,
lower point a foot, top a narrow neck; blink bar at 0.7 hf. Rule: crown 2x neck, 0.5x
its width tall; brim 1.3x crown, 1 px curl each end; coal; hat snaps down 1 beat late.

**Roller: one huge button boot.** Bare: an 8-point star wheel on a hub. 1860s-90s side-
button ankle boot, pointed toe, stacked heel; tell = dot row up the side. Misfit: one boot,
eight points; it hops, can't roll. New body: star stops, one point grows a 0.5 hf peg leg,
seven close into a crest fan above. Rule: boot 3x star width long, 0.6x tall, heel 0.15x
length, 6-8 brass dots 1 px; hop every 1.2 s, 0.2 hf, snap down.

**Needle: deerstalker.** Bare: a 1.9 hf double-pointed pitching spine with barbs. Tweed
cap, peaks front and back, flaps tied up; tell = two peaks. Misfit: skewered on the rear
tip, flaps loose. New body: spine pitches up 70 deg onto its rear barb, front tip thrust
like a beak, 3 barbs a side. Rule: cap 0.3x spine, tip through a cut hole; peaks 0.4x
cap; flaps 1.2x spine, camel lining, swinging 1 frame late.

**Knot: crinolette cage.** Bare: two crossed ellipse rings counter-turning. Crinolette
(1868-75): flat front, rear half hoops of steel on tapes; tell = open stacked hoops.
Misfit: worn as kin, a third ring bigger than both; it rolls inside it. New body: rings
uncross and stack, lower a wheel on the deck, upper a halo. Rule: cage 1.3x ring width,
5 hoops gunmetal 1 px, 2 vertical tapes, backdrop through; rolls 1 slot per beat.
