# Phase 3 · Eldritch lore research

Researched 2026-09-25 (one web pass). Summaries in our own words; ideas
only, no names or terms from these works go on the site. What we built
from it: `.claude/lore/eldritch.md`.

## Digest

### Lovecraft
- **Azathoth and its court** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azathoth,
  https://lovecraft.fandom.com/wiki/Court_of_Azathoth). The biggest thing
  is mindless at the centre; lesser beings keep up endless music around
  it without knowing why. → tier 2 keeps customs whose purpose is tier 3.
- **Great Old Ones vs Outer Gods**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cthulhu_Mythos_deities). Huge but local
  beings with cults, versus cosmic principles that notice no one. → our
  tier 2 vs tier 3 split.
- **Nyarlathotep** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyarlathotep). The one
  that walks among people in a tall, charming guise. → an old one whose
  only face is its outfit (the headless gentleman).

### Weird fiction
- **Ligotti, The Conspiracy Against the Human Race**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Conspiracy_Against_the_Human_Race).
  Beings as puppets of drives they never chose. → tier 2 thinks it
  chooses; its moods follow tier 3's tides.
- **Blackwood, The Willows** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Willows_(story)).
  The presence is never seen, only its signs: a hum, small pits in the
  sand, things missing, a veil "worn thin". → tier 3 as marks and sounds.
- **Blackwood, The Wendigo**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wendigo_(short_story)). Strides that
  lengthen, wind, a voice. → a pull that changes a gait before anything
  is seen.
- **Strugatsky, Roadside Picnic** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadside_Picnic).
  Visitors stop, never notice the locals, leave litter that becomes
  deadly zones and treasures. → tier 3's leavings are tier 2's relics.

### Games and tabletop
- **Control: the Board, the Former, Objects of Power**
  (https://control.fandom.com/wiki/The_Board,
  https://control.fandom.com/wiki/Former). A higher power acts through
  agents and ordinary objects; members can fall. → power in plain things.
- **Control Resonant** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_Resonant).
  The higher power recruits a go-between and never acts itself.
- **Dishonored: the Void, the Outsider**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Outsider_(Dishonored),
  https://dishonored.fandom.com/wiki/Bone_Charms). The Void reaches the
  world only through marks and carved charms; those who grab at it
  directly come to harm. → made objects as the way power passes down.
- **Bloodborne: Insight** (https://bloodborne.fandom.com/wiki/Insight).
  Things present all along become visible only to those who know more,
  at a cost to the mind. → the rare old one who starts to notice, and is
  shunned.
- **Planescape** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planescape). A hub city
  ruled by a presence that governs by leaving it alone. → the bridge as
  the crossing place.
- **Warhammer 40k: the Warp**
  (https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Immaterium). Mortal feeling pools
  into currents that feed beings. → feeling flows between tiers as
  weather.

### Scale and perception
- **Flatland; Hinton's fourth dimension**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatland,
  https://publicdomainreview.org/essay/notes-on-the-fourth-dimension).
  A higher being seen from below is only a slice that grows and vanishes;
  Victorians tied the fourth dimension to ghosts. → tier 2 is the
  fourth-dimension layer; tier 1 sees slices of it.
- **Stapledon, Star Maker** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Maker).
  Mind at ever larger scales; the maker looks on coldly. → tier 3 as a
  scale, not a monster; the cell and the body.
- **Watts, Blindsight** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blindsight_(Watts_novel)).
  Intelligence without awareness. → tier 3's acts as reflexes.
- **qntm, There Is No Antimemetics Division**
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Is_No_Antimemetics_Division).
  Things that resist being remembered. → knowledge of tier 3 does not
  stick; no guardian needed.
- **Annihilation's Shimmer** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annihilation_(film)).
  A zone that refracts everything, not cruel, just so. → tier 3 as
  environment.

### Clothes and social history
- **Victorian second-hand trade, the Rag Fair**
  (https://www.victorianlondon.org/clothing/secondhand.htm,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petticoat_Lane_Market). Clothes ran down
  a chain (owner, dealer, pawnbroker, stall, rags), altered each step;
  old-clothes men bought door to door. → every coat has a road; fit
  worsens down the chain; an old-clothes dealer on the bridge.
- **Servants' perquisites**
  (https://www.edwardianpromenade.com/servants-2/the-duties-of-a-ladys-maid/).
  Maids got cast-offs a season late; furs and fine lace only after a
  death. → fine cloth only reaches below when a high one falls.
- **Mourning dress** (https://www.lancasterhistory.org/victorian-mourning-dress/).
  Black crape everywhere; keeping it after mourning was unlucky, so it
  was cleared out. → much of the bridge's black is cleared mourning.
- **Veblen, The Theory of the Leisure Class, 1899**
  (https://brocku.ca/MeadProject/Veblen/Veblen_1899/Veblen_1899_07.html).
  Dress proves rank by showing waste and idleness. → rank as what you
  wear; the tailored suit as the highest waste.
