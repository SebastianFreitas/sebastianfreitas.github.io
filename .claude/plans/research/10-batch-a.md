# Phase 10 research: wardrobe batch A (east, large + carers)
Rows 3-9 of `05-wardrobe.md`. All: one flat fill per garment plus one tell
that survives 40-80 px. hf = body height fraction. All seven misfit.

**Tower: stovepipe + astrakhan ruff.** 1840s-50s stovepipe: straight, very
tall crown, narrow brim; height is the read. Astrakhan = tight curls, reads as
a bumpy edge. Misfit: hat on the neck band, a sky gap above the top.
Rule: crown 6x top height, 0.9x band width, sides bowed out 8% mid-height;
brim 1.25x crown width, 0.08x crown height; gap 0.15x crown width. Ruff 5-7
semicircle bumps r = 0.12x band width, one tone dark. Hat lags rock 1 frame.

**Mound: dolman mantle, sealskin hem.** 1870s-80s coat-cape, cape-sleeves sewn
into the side seams: batwing drop from the shoulders. Sealskin = dark glossy
brown short fur. Misfit: eye hole, hem trails as a train.
Rule: mantle from crown ridge down; hem 2x body width, pooled 0.1x hf tall,
trailing 0.4x body width behind. Seal band = bottom 15%, one tone darker, one
1 px highlight. Eye hole = eye r + 15%, 1 px darker rim.

**Eye: monocle on a chain.** Rim plus a sagging cord is what makes it a
monocle, not a ring. Misfit: rim wider than the pupil, chained to the stalk.
Rule: rim r 1.3x pupil, stroke 0.06x r (min 1 px), pewter; one linen crescent
glint at 10 o'clock, 0.3x r. Chain 5-7 dots, catenary sag 0.4x rim-to-stalk.
Rim follows the pupil 1 beat late, overshoot under 10%.

**Chime: fur earmuffs + striped comforter.** Earmuffs (1870s patent): two fur
pads on a thin band. Comforter = long knitted scarf, bold stripes, knitted
fringe. Misfit: muffs clamp a ring with no ears; scarf 3x too long.
Rule: pads r 0.35x top-ring height on its side edges, 1 px arc band. Scarf: 2
wraps between rings, two tails 3x wrap width to the deck; bottle/camel stripes
0.5x scarf width each; 3-4 fringe ticks at tail ends.

**Bundle: one fur muff.** Fur tube for both hands, hung on a long chain. Tell:
a fat rounded cylinder with dark open ends. Misfit: 7 lobes, 2 left out.
Rule: muff = rounded rect as big as the core, corner r 0.4x height, sable;
end ellipses darker, 0.3x muff width. Five lobes poke 0.2x lobe length out of
the ends; the two outside jitter 1 px (cold).

**Comb: spats + Ulster capelet.** Spats cover instep and ankle, pale, side
buttons. Ulster (coachman's coat): shoulder cape ending just past the elbow.
Misfit: capelet slid rearward on a bar with no elbows; spats 2x tall.
Rule: spat band 0.3x tooth length, linen, 1 px darker button dot outside;
wave phase step 0.7 rad/tooth. Capelet: tweed half-ellipse 0.45x bar length,
centred 60% toward the rear, hem 0.35x bar height below the bar.

**Mass: knitted muffler.** Plain ribbed wool neck scarf. Misfit: 4x body
length, both tails drag; it steps on one and freezes.
Rule: width 0.12x hf, oxblood, 3-4 darker rib lines per tail; one loop on the
ledge, tails 2x body length each, last 60% flat on the deck. Step-on: tail
pinned, body stops 0.5 s.
