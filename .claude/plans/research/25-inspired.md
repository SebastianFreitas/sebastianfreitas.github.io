# 25: inspired by the soul kind (research for beats: the change at the womb, worlds made inside, souls remade, the leaving)

## Sources

- Lem, "Non Serviam" (A Perfect Vacuum) / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Perfect_Vacuum : beings raised inside a made world argue about a maker they cannot see. The maker keeps the switch and owes them nothing he admits to. The old ones should read as that kind of maker: exact, patient, cold.
- Lem, personoids / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personoid : the made minds live in logic, not space. A world inside the body needs no sky of its own, only rules. We can draw it as ruled lines and ticks, not landscapes.
- Egan, Diaspora, "Orphanogenesis" / https://www.gregegan.net/DIASPORA/01/Orphanogenesis.html : a mind grown from a seed in a non-sentient womb-machine that can reach in and fix a flaw. Close to our remaking of souls, done by process, not by spell.
- Egan, Permutation City / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutation_City : a universe seeded with bare rules (the Autoverse) grows life. Its people explain their world without makers and refuse the true story. A world that forgets its makers is exactly what our captions cannot see.
- Bostrom, simulation argument / https://simulation-argument.com/ : makers run many copies of worlds, not one. Draw rows of worlds, not a single showpiece, so the scale reads as industry.
- Plato, Myth of Er / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myth_of_Er : souls line up, draw lots, choose a life from what is laid out, drink from Lethe and forget. Laying out lives like cloth on a counter is our strongest bridge to tailoring.
- Spindle of Necessity / https://www.greekmythology.com/Myths/The_Myths/Myth_of_Er/myth_of_er.html : the cosmos as one spindle of nested whorls, turned by Necessity. A flat disc stack we can rotate cheaply.
- The Moirai / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fates : spin, measure, cut. Three hand jobs, three props (spindle, rod, shears). Give them to three old ones and a life becomes a thread passing down a line.
- Clotho / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clotho : spinning as the start of a life. A spun soul-thread is a line we can draw growing, frame by frame.
- Bardo / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardo : the space between lives, full of coloured lights the dead mistake for gods. Our souls between runs should pass through such a gap, tiny and lost.
- Bhavacakra (Wheel of Life) / https://www.britannica.com/topic/bhava-cakra : rebirth drawn as a wheel with sectors of realms. A wheel is a machine; the old ones can build it with a crank.
- Lurianic Kabbalah, shevirat ha-kelim / https://newkabbalah.com/theosophical-kabbalah/lurianic-symbols/shevirat-ha-kelim-breaking-of-the-vessels/ : vessels break, sparks fall into husks. Pulling souls apart is our own breaking; the souls later "finding magic" is their tikkun, the sparks raised again.
- Tikkun / https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/tikkun-in-lurianic-kabbalah/ : repair happens from below, by the small ones, not the makers. Magic must be found by the souls, never handed down.
- Gnostic demiurge / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiurge : a craftsman god makes matter and sets rulers over the spheres, blind to what is above him. Our old ones are demiurges who know exactly what is above them.
- Borges, "The Circular Ruins" / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Circular_Ruins : a man is dreamed organ by organ, heart first, then the dreamer learns he is dreamed. Build a soul heart-first; keep the hint that the makers are made too.
- Borges, "The Library of Babel" / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Library_of_Babel : endless identical hexagon cells. A clean tiling for rows of small universes.
- Borges, "Tlon, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius" / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tl%C3%B6n,_Uqbar,_Orbis_Tertius : a secret society writes a world until it leaks into the real one; hronir copy lost things. Remade souls are hronir: copies of souls taken apart, a little larger.
- Calvino, Cosmicomics, "All at One Point" / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qfwfq : everyone packed into one point, gossiping. A tone model: the cosmic told as household, which suits our tailors.
- Stapledon, Star Maker / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Maker : a maker builds universe after universe, each a draft, some musical, some without space. Our worlds inside the body are drafts; some are thrown out.
- Chiang, "The Lifecycle of Software Objects" / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lifecycle_of_Software_Objects : made minds need years of raising, not a switch. Time spent is the cost; show many cycles, not one.
- Dark City, the Strangers' tuning / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_City_(1998_film) : pale men in long coats stop a city at midnight and reshape it by will through an underground machine. Closest film image to our coated old ones working a world; but ours use hands and tools, not will.
- Men in Black, the marble ending / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_(1997_film) : pull back until a galaxy is a marble in a giant's hand, then it goes in a bag. The pull-back reveal for "the universe is inside the body".
- Horton Hears a Who! / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horton_Hears_a_Who! : a whole town on a speck on a clover. Scale flip in one prop; the tiny world is only visible when the big one leans close.
- Outer Wilds, Eye of the Universe / https://outerwilds.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Universe : travellers sit at a fire and a new universe starts. Birth of a world as a quiet, small act.
- Grand orrery / https://www.whipplemuseum.cam.ac.uk/explore-whipple-collections/astronomy/grand-orrery : a crank-turned model of the heavens. The canonical "world made by hand": brass arms, flat discs, lamp as sun.
- Joseph Wright, A Philosopher Lecturing on the Orrery / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Philosopher_Lecturing_on_the_Orrery : faces lit from one lamp inside the model. Light from inside the made world onto the makers' faces, a good break from our left light.
- Jacquard loom / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine : punched cards drive the pattern; the card chain is a program. Law, not magic: the souls' lives as a card chain feeding a loom.
- Victorian tailoring tools / https://www.ernestwright.co.uk/the-historical-tailor/ : shears for cloth never cut paper; chalk, tape, basting before fitting. Every step of a fitting maps onto remaking a soul.
- Bespoke tailoring / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bespoke_tailoring : cut to one body from a drafted pattern. Each old one's own coat is bespoke; the souls get ready-to-wear.
- Brothers Quay, Street of Crocodiles / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_of_Crocodiles_(film) : a puppet is pulled into a tailor's shop, measured and given a new head by doll-assistants. Dust, pins, pulleys; the exact mood for a soul-fitting.
- Kentridge, Drawings for Projection / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Kentridge : one drawing erased and redrawn, leaving ghosts of old states. Each life of a soul leaves a faint trace; cheap on canvas as low-alpha shapes kept.
- Lem, Solaris / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solaris_(novel) : an ocean makes copies (mimoids) and visitors out of memory. The Primordisentia can mimic the old ones' shapes back at them.
- Hollow Knight, the vessels / https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Vessels : a king breeds thousands of empty vessels in an abyss and keeps the one that seems pure; the rest fall. Selection with waste; the discard pile is the horror.
- Inside, the Huddle / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_(video_game) : a mass of bodies fused in a lab tank. A flat silhouette of many limbs in one shape; our Primordisentia is kin.
- Gris / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gris : colour returns to a grey world area by area. The souls finding magic can be the first colour ever inside a made world.
- Journey, the ending / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game) : a light leaves the summit and runs back down the path. The leaving in reverse: lights retrace where they came from before scattering.
- Moebius, The Airtight Garage / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airtight_Garage : an asteroid holding a pocket universe, run by a major with generator engines, on three levels. A made world with engines at its edge, overseen from outside.
- Queen Mary's Dolls' House / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary%27s_Dolls%27_House : working lifts, water and light at 1:12. Made worlds that work because they are built right, not because they are wished.
- Ships in bottles / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_in_a_bottle : masts folded flat, pushed through the neck, pulled up by a thread. The way in to the body: fold the thing, push it through a mouth, raise it inside.
- Haeckel, Kunstformen der Natur / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunstformen_der_Natur : radiolaria as perfect symmetric cages. A remade soul could look like one: a small lattice, too regular to be born.
- Kurzgesagt / https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurzgesagt : flat cosmology that reads at thumbnail size. Keep each beat to one legible idea and one moving part.

## Ideas for the beats

1. **The dressing mirror.** An old one stands before a tall oval of the Primordisentia's surface; its shape in the surface is a soul-shape. It pulls its own outline in to match, then a coat falls on from above (hung, not conjured). One pushed garment, as art-style says.
2. **Pattern on the dark.** A pale chalk line draws itself across the backdrop around a body; shears follow the chalk; the cut piece is lifted away and hung. Used first on themselves (the coat), later on souls: the same motion, a darker meaning.
3. **Ship through the mouth.** A folded world (flat discs stacked like a closed fan) is pushed by a long rod into a slit in the Primordisentia; a thread pulls; inside, the discs open into an orrery.
4. **The orrery shop.** Pull back from a tiny turning world to a crank in a gloved hand, then to a row of old ones each cranking one, like a counting house. Many worlds, one aisle (Library of Babel tiling).
5. **Law cards.** A chain of punched cards feeds a loom-shaped frame; each card that passes adds one rule to the world inside (a ring appears, an orbit locks, a sea levels flat). No glow, only click and set.
6. **The pull-apart.** A clot of devouring souls is held in tongs; two old ones pull; it separates into pale threads, some snap and fall. Only the sound of it, no gore: flat strands on dark.
7. **Remade, heart first.** One kept piece is set on a counter; a small bright core first, then a lattice around it (Haeckel symmetry), then a thin cloth over it. A soul made like a garment on a form.
8. **Spin, measure, cut.** Three old ones in a line: one spins a thread off a remade soul, one lays a rod along it, one closes shears. The cut end drops into a world below as a spark. Repeat faster, faster: lives as a conveyor.
9. **The wheel.** A rebirth wheel built as a mill wheel: souls ride it down into a world, vanish, come back up paler, go round again. The old ones check each one with a loupe on the way up.
10. **Lethe as a rinse.** Between runs each soul passes through a thin flat sheet of dark water held by two hands; it comes out blank. Cheap: a horizontal band with an alpha wipe.
11. **Palimpsest souls.** Each return leaves a faint ghost ring around the soul (Kentridge traces). After many runs one soul carries a halo of old lives; that is the one that finds it.
12. **The first colour.** Inside a grey world, one soul does something unscripted and a single warm colour appears (not the cold rift light). Every old one in the aisle stops cranking at once and turns its head.
13. **The fitting ends.** Having what they wanted, they take off their aprons, fold their tools, and the shop lamps go out one by one, left to right, against the light.
14. **Coats into the void.** The old ones walk off in several directions, each a small dark silhouette with one bright pin of their own; the pins spread like departing ship lamps, then the frame holds on the Primordisentia, still turning, still full of made worlds.
15. **Journey reversal.** Before leaving, each one's light runs back along the path they came (womb, shop, aisle) in one sweep, then scatters; a quiet goodbye without a face.

## Caption voice notes

- The record sees only what reached it: "worlds were made", never who cranked them. It does not know about the old ones, the shop, the cards.
- Short and plain, one clause, often starting "And". "We are told the dark was measured." "Some were taken apart. Some were kept."
- Crafts appear as verbs the record could feel from inside: measured, cut, turned, sent, sent again. Not tools, not makers.
- Rebirth from below: "We lived. We forgot. We lived again." The record counts lives, never runs or cycles.
- Magic is found, not given: "And one of us remembered a thing no one had taught." Never "magic" as a spell word if the site avoids it; "the old way", "what we had lost".
- The leaving is felt as a loss of weight or attention: "And the hands were gone. The turning went on." Never "they left" with a subject named.
- No numbers it could not know, no scale words ("infinite", "universe"); prefer "all of it", "the dark", "the lamps".
