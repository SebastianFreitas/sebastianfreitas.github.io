1. Wash: the smears carry a fine wavy wood-grain texture (fine noise) and sit as isolated pale patches; the ground between them is flat warm taupe, not the refs' soft cool smoke.
2. Ink top clusters are cut off flat by the panel's top edge and sit over the label, reading as round dots, not spray.
3. Tears C's big regular sawtooth ends read as ribbon banners; tears A is too tame to read as torn.
