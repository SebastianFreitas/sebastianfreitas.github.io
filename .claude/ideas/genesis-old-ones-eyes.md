# Idea: Genesis, through an old one's eyes

Status: idea only (owner, 2026-10-28). Not a plan yet. Start it with
`/plan new genesis-old-ones-eyes: <this file>`.

## The shift

The Genesis cutscene currently reads as *the* beginning of the world. The
owner wants it to read as *a* beginning: one old one's memory of how
things began, told from where that creature stood. Other creatures would
remember it differently. The visitor should leave feeling they saw one
witness's account, not the official creation myth.

## Goals

1. **Full relabelling pass.** Every caption, beat name, title card, HUD
   line, segment label and the glimpse card's wording is rewritten from
   the old one's point of view: what it saw, felt and misunderstood, with
   no claim to be the whole truth. Words like "the beginning" and "the
   first" go; "what I remember", "before me", "from where I stood" come
   in. Code names (beat ids, `birth`, storage keys) stay so nothing
   breaks; only what the visitor reads changes.
2. **The author narrates.** The captions become the author's voice: he
   is the one telling this old one's story. One consistent narrator
   across every segment.
3. **We see the author.** He appears in most scenes as a small, steady
   presence (at the edge, watching, writing, listening to the old one),
   so the visitor understands who is telling it. He never blocks the
   action and never fights or speaks over the creatures.

## Open questions for the interview

- Who is the author in the setting, and what does he look like (an
  existing model, or a new asset; art-style.md applies)?
- Which old one is the witness? Does the cutscene show its body, or only
  its view?
- Does the narration name the old one, or keep it nameless?
- Is the author in every segment, or only the bridge-beacon one first
  (the first segment is the current focus)?
- Are the captions rewritten by the owner, or drafted for the owner to
  approve?

## Keep

- The old ones' shapes and the current art (see the memories on old ones,
  ceiling clouds and the hole burst). This is a story and labelling
  change plus one new character, not a redraw.
- The first segment's timing (about 34 s) unless a new caption needs time.
