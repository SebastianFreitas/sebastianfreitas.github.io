Eastern reach

Rex

Not a place — pieces of one. Rex Immotus stood against a fleeting evil and lost, and dying he left his body to be used. Every soul since got a vessel out of it. What's left is three masses, each one larger than a system, and each cut the same way: a surface, the caves under it, and something below that.

The largest carries mountains with a castle nobody has cleared in an age, and far east of it the nocturnals still hold a kingdom. Everything between is a war — species out of the void, and the wrecks of whatever carried them here. Its lowest layer is hell, and hell is the only part of Rex the Root can reach.

The Root

The first mass of souls, and it never stopped being one. Every arm of it is climbing, pressed hard against the next, and none of them have found a top yet. Everything east of here has already happened — it has simply been happening in the same place long enough to grow.

Older than any of it. It once took the shape of an observer and did nothing but look — into the first states of a life, inside the womb, before there was anything to name. It has since found other work. What it does now is hold the void off Rex, and it does not appear to have been asked.

Western reach

The Mainland

This ground was made by a war — pure souls against corrupted ones, and those who watched called them angels and demons. Out of the angels came the first mortal, pure angel blood, who caged demons and took what they had. That was only where it started: he was the first of the five warlocks born here. What stands on it now is a city holding civilians in the quintillions, and Libertech has never finished counting them.
