Western reach

The Unwritten

West, the hours run thin. I have seen a mainland man born, grown and buried while Rex turned its face once. Further west the reckoning worsens. Further still I will not pretend to reckon.

Nothing is written here, and that is no fault in the record. Ships are set west, they cease to answer, and we enter them not as lost but as future.

The Bridge

Before it there was no law. Then there was one: a line laid through the churn that would not move when all else moved. Those who found it chose a hand, left or right, and walked. That choosing is why you may say west and mean anything by it.

It runs through the whole of what is real. Lay your palm on it. It is cold, it is solid, it does not answer. It holds because it must, and that is the entire body of what is known.

Open dark

The Void

Do not call it distance. Distance is a mercy. Distance says there are two things with a space between them. The Void asks whether there are two things at all and never closes the question, and chaos is what moves through it.

Men cross. Most are not returned. Those returned are seldom the men who set out, and the difference has no name you could lay a finger on.

Eastern reach

Rex

Not a place. Pieces of one. Rex Immotus stood against a thing that was only passing through, and lost, and dying left his body open. Every soul that has worn flesh has worn his.

He is cut in three. The surface, which the Fall broke and has not finished breaking. The underground, where most living things keep, for the sun is unkind and the ground was the only roof when the ships came down. Beneath that, hell, the one door Rex keeps to the Root. Edgefall you will hear named often. It sits in a wound, the hole Obrokxus tore going out.

The Root

The first mass of souls, and it has never stopped being one. It was the womb. Everything that wears a body was pressed into shape inside it, and nothing ever told it to cease. It is a wall of red meat with no top, every arm still climbing, each pressed hard against the next.

The proper way out is to be carried. The gods reach in, take what is unfinished, set it down into a new life. The Vorgath found another. Skinless, in pain that does not relent, healing faster than they can be put down, rotting what they touch into more of themselves, they force the door, and the door opens into hell.

Overhead

The Watcher

They will tell you it is the sun, and they are not lying. It rises, it burns, it is no friend to skin.

Before there was anything worth naming it watched the first states of a life from inside the womb, and did nothing else. It reaches in now. Against the Administration, against the Colors sent out to keep their order, it stands. You have no business surviving out here. You endure because something ancient decided you would, and none of us know what it wants.

Western reach

The Mainland

This ground was won in a war of souls: those that held whole against those the Void had opened. Watchers named them angels and demons. The names took, and took badly. The whole ones were Seraphin. The others were Malgrur, and out of the Malgrur came devils.

The first mortal born here was got of both, of Ormius and of Ava, and nothing in him was pure. Seraphin taught him, Malgrur taught him, and both his parents taught him with their own mouths. One of the two meant to keep him for an instrument and did not get what he paid for. He was Mordrial, first of five. What stands now is one city without a seam, and the counting of its people has never overtaken the people.

Signal · sector zero

Sector Zero

Engine Unreal 5Language C++ / BlueprintRole Solo, everythingStatus Proof of concept

A first-person horror game set in one room, where a text file is the physics engine. You play Brian, sent into a dead research site with no memory of arriving. The terminal, RealityTXT, holds a record of every object in the room, and whatever the record says, reality obeys. Brian has a record too.

Small on purpose. One room means every object can be built until it feels real, from the chair you drive around to the lantern that lies about working. The storm takes what you have not registered. The game explains almost nothing, and the answer is rarely the obvious one.
